# Easy Rumours

Guides one Hunters' Rumour at a time and keeps your rumour stats.

## What it does

- **Tells you the next step**: get a rumour, fetch the items, travel, hunt, head back, hand in. One line says what to do now and the step after it is shown in orange.
- **Highlights what you need**: your hunter, the teleport to use, the guild stairs, the creatures, traps to check and spots to set, and the items to grab from the bank or your huntsman's kit.
- **Picks a route**: walking, the quetzal whistle, Fortis teleports, fairy rings or your house, from the settings you tick under *Your house*.
- **Counts catches against the pity count** (reduced for each guild hunter outfit piece you wear), and shows a rainbow tint when the rare part drops.
- **Wyrmscraig goats**: highlights the goats you can Telekinetic Grab into the pit and the best tile to stand on.
- **Stats sidebar**: rumours this session, rumours per hour, time per rumour and your history.

The current rumour is read from your hunter's dialogue and the quetzal whistle's message, so talk to your hunter (or check the whistle) once after installing.

The plugin never clicks for you, uses no reflection and makes no network requests.
